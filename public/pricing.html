Why our pricing is structured this way

FMC NextPage is engineered for organizations that treat learning as a strategic capability. We do not offer a free tier because our product investments are focused on delivering enterprise-grade reliability, compliance, and human support from day one. This enables us to deliver predictable outcomes: fast onboarding, secure data handling, measurable learner outcomes, and reduced operational overhead.

The Start → Diamond progression matches common adoption patterns. Start is designed for professional launches that require reliable infrastructure and predictable seat packages at $515 per month. Pro adds richer analytics, team seats and SSO for growing teams. Gold and Premium increase capacity and assurance through technical account management, performance prioritization and contractual SLAs. Diamond is a bespoke offering for global rollouts with dedicated engineering and legal packaging to meet procurement and regulatory needs.

Choosing the right plan depends on goals: are you monetizing content, centralizing corporate training, or rolling out regulated compliance programs? For creators and small teams, Pro is often the sweet spot — it provides the analytics and integrations that materially improve conversion and retention. For organizations that need guaranteed uptime, audit trails and legal terms, Premium and Diamond provide contractual assurances and white-glove onboarding to remove migration risk.

What your subscription pays for

Subscriptions fund platform operations, continuous product development, security investments and the human support that accelerates your time to value. Practical items covered by subscription revenue include:

  • Distributed CDN and multi-region caching to keep content fast for learners around the world.
  • Regular security scanning, patching and encryption practices to protect learner and organizational data.
  • Analytics infrastructure that processes engagement, conversions and cohort trends at scale.
  • Ongoing engineering to add integrations, reporting exports and accessibility improvements.
  • Human support and onboarding to reduce your time-to-launch and enable immediate product adoption.

Multi-currency and billing transparency

To reduce friction for international teams we display list prices in USD and EUR. The EUR amounts shown are for guidance (conversion is an estimate); final billed amounts depend on the payment provider and exchange rates at the time of payment. For Business and Diamond customers we issue invoices and can negotiate billing terms such as net-30 or net-60 where appropriate.

Return on investment (ROI)

The best way to think about ROI is to map subscription cost to concrete outcomes like reduced time-to-onboard, fewer compliance failures, or revenue generated through paid courses. For example, a small course provider on Pro who converts 5% of a 2,000 visitor campaign at an average price of $149 will recover subscription investment quickly and have significant margin for reinvestment. For enterprises, automating training management and reporting often decreases manual administrative labor and speeds compliance audits — these operational savings frequently justify Premium or Diamond engagements.

Billing, taxes, and cancellation

We support monthly and yearly billing; yearly billing typically receives a standard 20% discount. Taxes such as VAT are calculated at checkout and displayed as separate invoice line items where applicable. Cancellation and refund policies vary by plan and contract; Business and Diamond customers will have contract language that governs termination, data export and handover.
