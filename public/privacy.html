3. Types of Personal Data We Collect

We process different categories of personal data depending on how you interact with our Services. The categories include (but are not limited to):

  • Identity & Account Data: name, username, email address, hashed password, profile photo (if uploaded), and any profile fields you choose to provide.
  • Contact & Billing: billing name, billing address, payment instrument details (tokenized by our payment processor — we do not store full card numbers), invoicing information and VAT or tax identifiers where required.
  • Transactional Data: records of purchases, downloads, subscriptions, payment confirmations and receipts, order history.
  • Communications: support requests, chat logs (if saved), email communications, and feedback you provide.
  • Usage & Technical Data: IP address (logged in limited circumstances), device and browser metadata, operating system, pages visited, session activity, timestamps and other technical diagnostic data.
  • Content & User-Generated Content: posts, comments, forum contributions, uploaded files or attachments, and publicly visible profile content.
  • Marketing & Preference Data: subscription preferences, promotional opt-in status, interaction with marketing emails and announcements.

We design our services to minimise the personal data we collect. For accounts created solely for exploring public content, minimal information (email and chosen username) may be required; for paid products, billing information will be collected as necessary to process payments.

4. How We Collect Personal Data

We collect personal data through a variety of means:

  • Directly from you: when you register an account, complete forms, subscribe to communications, make purchases, upload content, or contact our support team.
  • Automatically: via logs, cookies and minimal analytics that collect aggregated, pseudonymised or non-identifying usage information.
  • From third parties: such as payment processors, identity verification services, advertising platforms, or social login providers when you choose to authenticate via such services.

We do not buy personal data from data brokers for marketing purposes.

5. Purposes of Processing & Lawful Bases

We process personal data for specific, legitimate purposes. For each purpose we rely on one or more lawful bases under the GDPR, commonly including:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide Services you have requested (for example to manage your account, process payments and deliver purchased content).
  • Legal compliance: to comply with applicable laws and regulatory obligations (for instance tax reporting).
  • Legitimate interests: for our legitimate business interests where they are not overridden by your rights (for example preventing fraud, improving our Services, and enforcing our Terms & Conditions).
  • Consent: where you have given explicit consent (e.g., marketing communications, cookie tracking beyond strictly necessary cookies).

Examples of processing activities and lawful bases

  • Account management (contractual): collecting the data necessary to create and maintain your account — lawful basis: performance of a contract.
  • Payment processing (contractual/legal): handling transactions via third-party payment processors — lawful basis: performance of contract and legal obligations regarding tax/financial record keeping.
  • Security & fraud prevention (legitimate interest): monitoring accounts for suspicious activity to protect users and the platform — lawful basis: legitimate interests.
  • Marketing communications (consent or legitimate interest): sending promotional emails where you have opted in or where allowed by law — lawful basis: consent or legitimate interest, depending on context and jurisdiction.

If we rely on consent for specific processing, you can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us or using the opt-out mechanism within the communication you received. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.

6. Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to provide core site functionality, remember preferences, and analyse usage. Cookies used by or on behalf of CapitalFlowX may include:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: required for core platform functionality (session management, authentication). These do not require consent.
  • Functional cookies: to remember user preferences (display choices, language).
  • Analytical cookies: used to collect minimal, aggregated analytics about site usage (see Analytics section).
  • Third-party cookies: placed by external service providers (for example video hosting, payment providers or marketing platforms) when you use embedded features or follow referral links.

You can control and manage cookie preferences through your browser settings and, where applicable, through our cookie and consent management tools. Disabling cookies may reduce functionality or degrade your experience on the Services.

7. Analytics & Minimal Anonymous Tracking

CapitalFlowX uses only minimal, aggregated and anonymised analytics strictly for service improvement and operational monitoring. Our analytics approach is intentionally privacy-preserving:

  • We collect non-identifying usage metrics such as page load timing, volume of unique sessions (as aggregated counts), most viewed courses, and feature usage statistics.
  • We avoid storing personally identifiable information in our analytics dataset; where identifiers are used for technical session management they are pseudonymised and not linked to your profile in analytics exports.
  • Where third-party analytics providers are used, we select vendors that support IP anonymisation and data processing agreements that meet GDPR standards. We limit the data we share with analytics providers to what is necessary for analytics purposes.

If you wish to opt out of analytics tracking, you may disable cookies in your browser or contact us to request that we exclude your account from analytics processing. Please note that opting out of analytics may reduce our ability to provide personalised content and to identify bugs or service issues that affect you.

We may share personal data with trusted third-party service providers acting as processors on our behalf where necessary to provide the Services. These may include:

We may disclose personal data to the extent necessary to:

9. International Transfers

CapitalFlowX is an international service. Personal data may be transferred to, stored and processed in countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA), including the United States. Where such transfers occur, we employ appropriate safeguards in accordance with applicable law:

  • We use standard contractual clauses (SCCs) or other GDPR-approved transfer mechanisms when transferring personal data to processors in jurisdictions that do not provide an adequacy decision;
  • We limit transfer of personal data to the minimum required for the provision of our Services;
  • We require subprocessors to implement adequate technical, organisational and contractual controls.

If you are located in the EU and require additional information about the safeguards we use for a specific transfer, please contact our privacy team at privacy@capitalflowx.example.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, to comply with legal or regulatory obligations, to resolve disputes, to enforce our agreements, and to maintain records for tax and accounting purposes. Specific retention periods may vary by category of data:

  • Account data: retained for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter to allow account recovery and for fraud prevention (e.g., up to 2 years after account closure, unless otherwise required by law).
  • Transactional & billing data: retained as required by tax and accounting laws (commonly 6–10 years depending on jurisdiction).
  • Support communications: retained for a period necessary to resolve requests and for quality improvement (commonly 2–5 years).
  • Analytics data: aggregated analytics retained in anonymised form; raw logs are archived and purged following operational policies (commonly within 90–540 days depending on the log type).

When feasible and appropriate, we anonymise or aggregate personal data so it is no longer personally identifiable, and we may retain such anonymised datasets indefinitely for analysis and improvement of our Services.

11. Security Measures

We maintain industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction or damage. Measures include (but are not limited to):

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS (HTTPS) and encryption at rest where appropriate;
  • Access controls and role-based permissions for internal systems;
  • Secure configuration of servers and infrastructure; vulnerability scanning and patch management;
  • Regular backups and disaster recovery plans;
  • Employee training and policies governing data access and handling.

While we endeavour to protect personal data, no security system is impenetrable. If we become aware of a security incident affecting personal data, we will act in accordance with legal obligations, including notifying affected persons and relevant supervisory authorities where required by law.

12. Your Rights under the GDPR

If you are located in the European Union, you have certain rights regarding your personal data. These rights may include:

  • Right of access: the right to obtain confirmation of whether we process your personal data and, where applicable, to receive a copy of that personal data.
  • Right to rectification: the right to have inaccurate personal data corrected.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): in certain circumstances, the right to request deletion of personal data.
  • Right to restriction of processing: to request limitation of processing in certain situations.
  • Right to data portability: to obtain and reuse certain personal data for your own purposes across different services.
  • Right to object: to object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.
  • Right to withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent, the right to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing).

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details in section 17. We will respond to requests in accordance with applicable law, typically within one month; where requests are complex or numerous, we may extend the response time and will inform you accordingly.
